- Pen input. Nothing here can drive a pen. It comes through the same pointer events as the mouse.
- A second finger (non-primary pointer) being ignored. The CDP steps send one touch point. The code checks `isPrimary`, but nothing drives a second pointer.
- `buttons` with a middle or right mouse button through render.sh. It has no such step; the arena test covers `[2]` refusing a left click, but not a right click starting one.
- `pointercancel` ending a drag. Nothing here can make the browser cancel a pointer.
- The benchmark with a live agent. It spends money and wasn't asked for. The arena program above is the free check.
